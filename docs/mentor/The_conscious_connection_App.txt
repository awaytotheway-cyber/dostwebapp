
The conscious connection App-Sonic Neuroplasticity.
Objective: To create a personalized coaching system called Conscious Connections that focuses on sonic neuroplasticity.
Plan of Action:
1. User Sign-up and Initial Data Collection
   - Gather user's demographics, place of birth, and family situation
   - Collect information on key goals the user wants to work on
   - Complete personality, enneagram, and Ayurvedic vata-pitta-kapha assessments
2. User Profile Creation
   - Identify the user's field of activities, proclivities, and conditioning
   - Determine thought processes and identifications with culture, gender, and job responsibilities
3. Persona and Voice Identification
   - Help users identify their "Traumatic persona" and "Shielding managers"
   - Guide users to recognize and name the "voices in the head which are loud"
   - Identify the user's suffering and how it manifests
4. Voice Recording and Analysis System
   - Provide prompts for users to express feelings and thoughts
   - Implement a recording system that blocks third-party voices and other noises
   - Allow for 1-to-1 sessions with a recording button or prompted by a name eg: Siri
   - Analyze recordings for tone, pitch, volume, and quality to determine mood
5. Belief Examination Process
   - Present user's conditioned beliefs
   - Challenge these beliefs through questioning ( the 5 why approach- but not limited to 5):
     - Ask why they feel the belief is true
     - Question if the belief applies to other people
     - Explore why they think others should conform to that belief
   - Continue challenging until the user recognizes the truth or myth of the belief
6. Habit and Language Analysis
   - Identify habits that alienate users from their true self
   - Analyze statements the user often makes, both consciously and unconsciously
   - Help users recognize their "coverings" (e.g., denial, shame, scarcity, pride, lust, lamentation)
7. Awareness Exercises
   - Implement exercises to increase user awareness
   - Provide feedback on what users learn throughout the course
8. Behavior and Vocabulary Modification
   - As users become more aware, guide them to change their behavior and vocabulary
   - Encourage users to become more conscious in their choices
9. Goal Alignment
   - Help users create the destiny they aspire to through conscious choice-making
