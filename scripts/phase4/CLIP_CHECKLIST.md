# The 48 clips — recording checklist

Tick each one as you record it. Print this, or keep it open on a
second screen.

**The extension does not matter.** `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.mp3`, `.opus`,
`.3gp` — whatever your recorder produces. Only the *name before the
dot* matters, and it is shown below without an extension for that
reason. Just keep whatever your phone gives you.

**Capitals, spaces and underscores are all fine.** `EN_Calm_Quiet_1`
reads the same as `en-calm-quiet-1`, and the order of the parts does
not matter either. Every name below was round-tripped through the
actual validation script, so these exact names are guaranteed to be
read correctly.

Put all 48 in one folder. Any clip whose name cannot be read is
skipped and listed, never silently miscounted.

---

## What to say

Say **the same sentence every time**, changing only *how* you say it.

| | Sentence |
|---|---|
| English | I have to go to the office tomorrow and finish the report. |
| Hindi | मुझे कल ऑफिस जाना है और रिपोर्ट पूरी करनी है। |
| Marathi | मला उद्या ऑफिसला जायचं आहे आणि रिपोर्ट पूर्ण करायचा आहे. |

## Before you start

- **Same phone, same recorder app, all 48 clips.**
- **Same distance** — about 20 cm, held the same way each time.
- **Turn off** noise suppression, voice enhancement and auto-gain.
  Automatic volume levelling is the worst offender: it flattens
  exactly the loudness differences being measured.
- Record the quiet and noisy versions of a language **in one sitting**,
  so your voice has not drifted between them.
- Leave a moment of silence at the start and end; don't clip the first
  word.

## The one rule that decides whether this is worth anything

**Mean it rather than perform it.** Lab emotion models fall apart in
real life because they learned from actors. If you ham it up, the
result measures your acting and looks better than the truth. Aim for
how you actually sound on a tired Tuesday, not a stage version of it.

If a take comes out not feeling like the state you meant, **delete it
or rename it to what it actually was**. An honestly mislabelled clip
is worse than no clip.

## When you're done

Put all 48 in one folder and tell me where. Then:

```bash
python3 scripts/phase4/validate_arousal.py path/to/clips/
```

It prints how many it scored and how many it skipped, so a
misnamed file shows up immediately rather than quietly dropping out
of the count.

## A fair warning before you spend the hour

These are deliberate recordings, not overheard speech. Even said
honestly they are cleaner than how you sound when you are not
thinking about it, so expect real listening sessions to score worse
than this will. And it is one voice — yours — which says nothing
certain about anyone else's.

A good result here means "worth building", not "accurate". A bad one
means the mock stays, and that is a real answer worth an hour rather
than a wasted one.
